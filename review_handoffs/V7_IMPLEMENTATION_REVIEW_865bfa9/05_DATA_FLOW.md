## Binding versus diagnostic boundaries

- `LIGHTGBM_CEILING`, shallow tree, sparse logistic, scorecard and RuleFit-lite are calculated in `run_model_diagnostics`. Raw prediction scores do not feed portfolio lanes.
- `LGBM_DISTILLED_LEAF`, `TREE_SINGLE_LEAF`, and `RULEFIT_LITE` can feed the simple-rule selector only after conversion to explicit conditions and common admission masks.
- Historical VAP 20/60/120 variables, POC/HVN diagnostics, formation shock controls and high-overhang summaries may appear in artifacts/reports without necessarily becoming a hard gate. `06_RULE_BINDING_MATRIX.csv` records each role.
- `finalize_result` reads previously generated artifacts to create text/JSON summaries; it is downstream of lane construction and does not alter the admissions already emitted.
