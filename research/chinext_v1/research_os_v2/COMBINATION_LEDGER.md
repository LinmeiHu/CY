## Eligibility rule

A combination enters this ledger only after each component has exploratory
evidence or a strong preregistered rationale, represents distinct information,
has a predicted interaction, and is specified before the outcome. Two components
are preferred. Every promising combination must compare baseline, A, B, and A+B,
including neither/A-only/B-only/both where estimable, and must preserve right-tail
and execution diagnostics.
