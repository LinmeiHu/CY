## Trend

- **FAMILY:** Trend.
- **HYPOTHESES_TESTED:** H-003; trend controls in H-004/H-020; index components
  in H-022.
- **REPRESENTATIONS_TESTED:** 60-day return, close/MA60, MA20 five-day slope,
  fraction of 20 days above MA20, MA20 state flips; other index return, MA
  distance, drawdown, and slope fields were screened descriptively.
- **ABSOLUTE_MEASURES_TESTED:** yes, primarily direction/strength/persistence.
- **PIT_NORMALIZED_MEASURES_TESTED:** none.
- **RELATIVE_MEASURES_TESTED:** 399102 versus 399006/CSI300 index returns and a
  Day-5 stock/399102 decomposition, not a complete market-trend map.
- **TIME_HORIZONS_TESTED:** primary 20/60-day state and short slope/persistence;
  library screens included 1/5/10/20/60/120-day returns and 5/10/20/60/120-day
  MA distances.
- **TRAJECTORIES_TESTED:** limited five-day slope and 20-day state persistence;
  no T-60..T-1 market trend-quality archaeology.
- **INTERACTIONS_TESTED:** breadth partialed on fixed trend controls; no formal
  trend x breadth synergy grid.
- **CONDITIONAL_STRUCTURE_TESTED:** only among entries already admitted by the
  binary MA20 market gate and in five fixed trend strata for breadth.
- **FAILURE_MODES_TESTED:** direction/magnitude, within-year, LOYO, block,
  opposite-tail, and fixed-control failures.
- **CROSS_YEAR_TESTED:** eight entry years and three blocks; absolute semantics
  were stable, but causal historical normalization was absent.
- **OOS_TESTED:** no untouched OOS for the broader family; all 2018-2025 outcomes
  are consumed.
- **WHAT_WAS_ACTUALLY_REJECTED:** H-003's exact medium-horizon entry-conditioned
  level/persistence prediction under its five primary representations.
- **WHAT_REMAINS_UNKNOWN:** quality, smoothness, drawdown within trend,
  persistence age, onset, multi-horizon alignment, strengthening/deterioration,
  causal extremeness, and incremental trend x breadth habitat.
- **CURRENT_FAMILY_STATUS:** `FAMILY_UNDEREXPLORED`.
- **CLOSURE_JUSTIFIED_YN:** `NO`.
