# CHINEXT V1 Research OS V2 handoff

Updated 2026-08-30.

## 1. What was studied?

The program validated the mandated worktree/branch/HEAD, reconstructed the two
prior CHINEXT V1 research programs, audited their hypotheses and experiment
registries, and inspected the frozen SuperMind V6 strategy's multi-representation
construction. No canonical strategy code, locked holdout, or prior research file
was modified.

## 2. What was learned?

Prior research established qualified breadth/opportunity evidence, strong
right-tail dependence, completed-path false-breakout topology, and descriptive
Day-3/Day-5 separation. It also produced many precise negative results. It did
not establish a deployable market gate, entry filter, conversion mechanism,
exit adaptation, chip rule, or breakout lineage rule.

## 3. What previous conclusions were narrowed?

H-003 remains rejected exactly as tested. The broader trend family is now
classified `FAMILY_UNDEREXPLORED`, because prior primary testing covered only
60-day return, MA60 distance, MA20 slope, 20-day above-MA persistence, and state
flips inside entries already admitted by the MA20 gate. Trend quality, age,
alignment, transition, and PIT historical normalization were not tested.

Likewise, the rejected fixed chip composite does not close all supply/demand
research, and rejected minimum-volume support does not close all support or
volume structure. These are scope corrections, not revisions of evidence.

## 4. Which market behaviors appear real?

- broad participation is associated with favorable opportunity paths;
- rare winners dominate V1 economics across years;
- false breakouts exhibit a robust completed-path opportunity-before-adversity
  sequence;
- severe losses and extreme winners become descriptively distinguishable early
  in the held path, without proven residual persistence.

## 5. Which mechanisms remain speculative?

Trend quality/age/transition, breadth divergence and acceleration, conditional
volatility, liquidity and risk-appetite habitats, leadership concentration,
formation sequences, and environment-by-setup interactions remain speculative.

## 6. What combinations were attempted?

Prior work tested fixed composites for breadth, pre-entry RS plus compression,
intraday signal-session acceptance, and chip-base coherence. It also tested a
breadth exposure rule whose outputs were later invalidated by provenance. No new
Research OS V2 combination has been tested.

## 7. Which combinations showed incremental synergy?

None has yet passed a complete `baseline/A/B/A+B` synergy design. Breadth retained
incremental MFE association after fixed trend controls, which supports treating
participation and direction as distinct habitat roles, not a finished
combination.

## 8. Which combinations failed and why?

The fixed RS/compression and chip-base composites failed raw/controlled or
coherence gates. The intraday acceptance composite was null. Breadth did not add
conversion/capture/giveback information after path/year/exit controls. The
persisted breadth exposure candidate is invalid evidence due to its unreconciled
replay-engine binding.

## 9. What market habitats appear relevant?

Broad participation appears relevant to opportunity formation. A usable habitat
map still needs absolute trend quality/age/transition, PIT historical context,
volatility structure, dispersion, liquidity, risk appetite, and leadership
concentration. No boundary is frozen.

## 10. Has a candidate strategy emerged?

No. Frozen CHINEXT V1 remains unchanged.

## 11. What evidence is missing?

The principal gaps are causal historical normalization, economically independent
trend representations, breadth depth/divergence/transition, conditionally tested
habitat interactions, matched controls, and future untouched validation.

AUDIT-ROSV2-M001 has now removed five-day minute availability as a blocker: all
1,995 event-sessions pass exact contract gates and provide 34 complete preview
descriptors. It also demonstrates enough redundancy to make raw feature screening
unsafe.

## 12. What should be studied next?

Preregister and execute one outcome-blind five-day representation freeze. Reduce
the preview panel to distinct supply-exhaustion, demand-strengthening, and
intraday-compression roles; require slope/endpoint/monotonicity stability and low
redundancy against each other and daily setup controls. Then build the independent
absolute/PIT-normalized trend map before any multi-scale outcome test.

## 13. Is a human decision required?

No. The next experiment uses existing registered/frozen public program inputs,
does not access CY-011, and does not modify canonical V1. Human authorization is
required only before locked validation or a production/canonical strategy change.
