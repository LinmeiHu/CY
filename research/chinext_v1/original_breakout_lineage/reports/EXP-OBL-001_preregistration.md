# EXP-OBL-001 preregistration

EXP-OBL-001 freezes a four-lineage, outcome-blind 2x2 hierarchy before any new-
program outcome reveal.

The two axes are:

1. base repair/compression, using fixed within-year ranks of prior-20 support
   shift, range contraction, and volatility contraction;
2. canonical-reference acceptance, using fixed within-year ranks of post-cross
   time above the prior-60 close reference, close retention, and resilience to
   trading back below the reference.

Each score is split at exactly 0.50; ties are HIGH. The four lineage IDs are
neutral structural labels. No future outcome is used to choose features, weights,
class count, split, or labels.

The complete scientific contract is the frozen
`experiments/EXP-OBL-001_spec.json`. A successful construction must cover all 399
events, keep every lineage at least 30 events and no more than 45% of the sample,
place at least two members of every lineage in every year, and achieve at least
65% exact assignment agreement under the fixed lower-complexity/base plus exact
five-minute neighbor.

Only after a valid, deterministic `LINEAGE_FREEZE_ID` is committed may a separate
outcome-reveal experiment be preregistered.
