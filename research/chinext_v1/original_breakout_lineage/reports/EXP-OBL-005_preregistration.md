# EXP-OBL-005 preregistration

EXP-OBL-005 freezes resistance-test topology without accessing an outcome.

The primary feature is the number of distinct entries into the fixed 2% zone
below the canonical prior-60 close reference. A continuous stay counts once;
leaving the zone and later returning creates another episode. This separates
repeated attacks from mere time spent consolidating near the high.

The exact same definition at 1% and 3% is frozen as neighbors. Zone-day counts,
maximum run, reference age, prebreakout distance, and breakout margin are context
only. The primary must vary in every year and correlate at least 0.65 with both
neighbors. No outcome file is available to the runner.

Only a deterministic feature freeze may authorize a later EXP-OBL-006 outcome
test. No trading rule or CY-011 access is authorized.
