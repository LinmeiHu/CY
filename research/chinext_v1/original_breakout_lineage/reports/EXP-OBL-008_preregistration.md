# EXP-OBL-008 preregistration

EXP-OBL-008 freezes a neutral selection-competition lineage before outcomes.
`L_CONTESTED` means the same-session candidate count strictly exceeds canonical
vacancies; `L_UNCONTESTED` means candidates fit within vacancies. The boundary
is the unchanged ten-position capacity rule, not a selected threshold.

All three bounded baseline blocks will be freshly replayed by the frozen engine
inside temporary directories. The parent constructor may read only event
ledgers and identity-only columns from the accepted 399-cycle ledger. It may not
read temporary execution, NAV, summary, or report files, any source-worktree
ledger, or any future outcome field.

Both lineages must contain at least 50 events, neither may exceed 85% of the
sample, and each must occur in at least six of eight years. Candidate/vacancy,
rank, cutoff-missingness, identity, PIT, and deterministic reconstruction must
all reconcile exactly. No outcome meaning, selection-pressure threshold,
strategy rule, V1 change, or CY-011 access is authorized.
