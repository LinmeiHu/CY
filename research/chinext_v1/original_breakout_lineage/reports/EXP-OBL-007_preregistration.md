# EXP-OBL-007 preregistration

EXP-OBL-007 tests the exact age of the canonical prior-60 close reference. The
predictor is the integer number of sessions from signal t back to the latest
occurrence of that reference. It was frozen outcome-blind in the EXP-OBL-003
formation artifact and was not an assignment axis of the rejected taxonomy.

The supply-turnover hypothesis predicts a positive continuous association with
both MFE and non-false-breakout. Raw rho must reach 0.10 and controlled partial
rank rho 0.08 for both endpoints, subject to BH, LOYO, temporal-block, tail,
boundary-age, duration/exit, security, and industry attacks. Fixed controls
include base width, T-1 positioning, signal breakout margin, V1 entry state, and
market state.

No age bin, threshold, direction change, strategy rule, canonical V1 change, or
CY-011 access is authorized. Terminal and right-tail results are secondary and
cannot rescue failed co-primary gates.
