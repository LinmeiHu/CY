# EXP-OBL-012 preregistration

EXP-OBL-012 is an outcome-blind construction test of objective prior-60 pivot
topology. A strict three-session centered low/high defines a trough/peak; ties are
not pivots. The signs of the log changes between the two latest troughs and the
two latest peaks create four neutral higher-low/lower-high combinations. No ID
has favorable or unfavorable outcome meaning.

The only fixed neighbor uses a strict five-session centered extremum. Every one
of 399 events must have at least two peaks and troughs under both definitions;
all four lineages must contain at least 25 events, no lineage may exceed 45%,
every lineage must occur in every year, and exact three-session/five-session
assignment agreement must reach 60%.

Only t-60 through t-1 action-safe adjusted highs/lows are permitted. Future
return, excursion, false-breakout, holding, exit, winner/loser, and all other
post-entry fields are forbidden. Failure closes this definition before outcomes;
success freezes it for a separate preregistered reveal. No threshold, rule, V1
change, or CY-011 access is authorized.
