# EXP-OBL-009 preregistration

EXP-OBL-009 is a clean outcome-blind feasibility refinement of EXP-OBL-008. It
preserves the engine, population, event-only access, exact contested capacity
boundary, all assignments, continuous context, timing, and outcome prohibition.
It uses fresh runner/output identities.

The only change is to the construction feasibility gate: minimum lineage size
changes from 50 to 40 and maximum lineage fraction from 85% to 90%. This was
justified before any outcome access because EXP-OBL-008 reconstructed a genuine
48-event contested class present in seven years while every identity, rank,
capacity, cutoff, temporal, and outcome-prohibition check passed.

No assignment, capacity boundary, threshold, favorable outcome meaning,
strategy rule, V1 change, or CY-011 access is authorized.
