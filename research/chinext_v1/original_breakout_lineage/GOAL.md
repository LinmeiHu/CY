# Original V1 breakout-lineage research goal

## Objective

Discover whether objectively different, point-in-time formation paths lead into
the canonical CHINEXT V1 breakout event and whether outcome-blind lineages have
economically and temporally stable differences in subsequent behavior.

The ordering is mandatory:

`event semantics -> outcome-blind representation -> lineage freeze -> outcome reveal -> falsification`

This program seeks reusable formation mechanisms, not a historically optimized
entry rule. Canonical V1 is not modified.

## Scientific boundary

- The research population and lineage assignments use only information available
  at or before the declared decision timestamp.
- Future return, MFE, MAE, false-breakout status, holding result, exit result,
  winner class, and every later observation are forbidden during lineage
  construction.
- Any full signal-session feature is available at `15:30 Asia/Shanghai` and can
  first inform a T+1 or later action. It cannot justify an earlier trade.
- Lineage definitions, assignment code, normalization, missing-data policy,
  inputs, and hashes are frozen under a `LINEAGE_FREEZE_ID` before outcomes are
  read.
- CY-011 2024-2026 validation remains locked and is not an input to this program.

## Completion standard

A mechanism becomes a candidate only after economically meaningful separation,
temporal stability, neighboring-definition robustness, plausible mechanism,
incrementality beyond canonical V1, strict PIT validity, feasible timing if
tradable, acceptable right-tail impact, and a skeptical multiple-testing
interpretation. Otherwise it is refined, rejected, or retained as descriptive.
