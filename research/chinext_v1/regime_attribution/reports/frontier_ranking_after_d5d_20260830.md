H-022 is refined, not validated. Its stock-specific core survives, but the fixed
beta-adjusted neighbor fails and HOLDOUT has no extreme-winner variation. Further
historical decomposition would raise search-history risk without resolving the
missing temporal evidence.

The severe-loss frontier is selected only for a coverage and endpoint-variation
audit. No horizon or feature is frozen until that audit shows a minimum-sufficient,
estimable design. A negative availability result returns the program to the
frontier rather than inventing data or relaxing endpoints.
