# Research frontier ranking after EXP-ECC-001

## Decision context

H-004 breadth is frozen for prospective validation. Pre-entry demand/compression,
industry-versus-stock relative strength, post-day-5 continuation, T+1 gap, and
same-day entry crowding have been rejected. H-016 still supports a completed-path
false-breakout sequence in which opportunity precedes adversity, but that sequence
has not been localized early enough to distinguish initial rejection from later
exit-conditioned deterioration.

## Ranked frontiers

| Rank | Frontier | Information value | PIT/data feasibility | Main risk | Decision |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Early held-path reversal and post-day-5 failure | Directly tests whether H-016 begins as an early impulse/giveback sequence and whether it precedes later failure. | Complete five-session action-safe coverage for 295 frozen survivors; accepted residual post-day-5 outcome exists. | Post-entry and outcome-consumed; cannot become an entry rule. | Select one continuous day-5 test. |
| 2 | Severe-loss formation | Explains a distinct downside tail not explained by breadth. | Outcomes and held paths available. | Severe losses are only 44 cycles and explain less V1 P&L than false breakouts; higher concentration risk. | Hold in reserve. |
| 3 | Canonical entry-score component attribution | Could identify redundancy inside V1's admitted entries. | Entry RS, box, volume, and min-volume fields exist. | High redundancy with frozen strategy logic and high data-mining risk after H-011/H-012. | Defer absent a sharper mechanism. |
| 4 | Portfolio concurrency/capital contention | Could explain portfolio-level opportunity loss. | Exact ledgers permit reconstruction. | Cohort crowding already failed; counterfactual allocation would approach strategy modification. | Defer. |
| 5 | Chip/supply-demand state | Potentially reusable structural mechanism. | Not yet proven under an accepted strict PIT lineage in this program. | High infrastructure cost and lineage risk. | Fail closed until governed data are demonstrated. |

## Selected minimum-sufficient question

Does close-to-close peak giveback accumulated by the fifth held session predict
additional return failure after day 5, beyond the observed day-5 return and fixed
pre-entry state?

This temporally ordered formulation avoids relabeling the completed H-016 path as
an entry signal. It tests exactly one primary feature at one already accepted
landmark. No threshold, horizon sweep, exit counterfactual, or strategy replay is
authorized.
