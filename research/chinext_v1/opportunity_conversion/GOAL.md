# CHINEXT V1 Opportunity-to-P&L Conversion Goal

## Objective

Without modifying authoritative CHINEXT V1, explain why market breadth
reliably describes entry opportunity, MFE, and right-tail availability but
does not reliably become terminal return or a tradable exposure overlay.

The required attribution chain is:

`Market Opportunity -> Entry Selection -> MFE Generation -> Holding Path -> MFE Capture -> Terminal P&L`

The final decision must identify whether the largest economically meaningful
loss is primarily:

- stock-selection inefficiency;
- failure to identify the right tail;
- premature exit;
- excessive post-MFE giveback;
- holding-period mismatch;
- false-positive entries;
- an interaction of those mechanisms; or
- no sufficiently stable and exploitable improvement space.

> Market breadth explains V1 entry opportunity and right-tail availability,
