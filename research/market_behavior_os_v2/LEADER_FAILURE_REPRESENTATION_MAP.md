# Leader-Failure Representation Map

Frozen before MKT-LDR-001. A high leadership-concentration level is not a leader
failure. This map asks whether deterioration can be represented from the two
already-frozen breadth roles without future paths, outcomes, or strategy labels.

## Economically distinct coordinates

| Concept | Primary | Fixed neighbors | Semantic boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concentration decay | negative five-session change in top-decile positive-return-mass concentration | same five-session change for top 5%/20%; three-/ten-session changes for top 10% | falling concentration can be rotation, diffusion, or leader loss; it is not failure by itself |
| Discovery deterioration | negative five-session change in net 60-session new-high/new-low participation | same five-session change for 40/80-session discovery; three-/ten-session changes for 60-session discovery | weakening discovery can be consolidation or breakdown; it is not leader failure by itself |
| Leadership/discovery imbalance | causal trailing-756-session percentile of top-decile concentration minus the corresponding percentile of net-60-session discovery | top5/net40 and top20/net80 percentile gaps | contextual narrow leadership relative to discovery, not a temporal failure |

The two raw accepted input levels remain in the output as the absolute economic
state. Concentration decay and discovery deterioration are absolute changes.
The imbalance is explicitly a PIT-context coordinate; it never replaces its raw
concentration/discovery pair.

## Conditional geometry

Joint leader-deterioration geometry may be described only if concentration decay
and discovery deterioration both pass the frozen coverage, neighboring-
definition, denominator, and year-cell gates. If either fails, MKT-LDR-001 must
not rescue the joint concept with a different horizon, threshold, smoother, or
best-looking quadrant.

All primaries preserve causal expanding/trailing percentiles, robust z-scores,
and governed-view relative coordinates. Views and denominators are inherited
unchanged from MKT-BRTH-002. No raw CY-006 data are reread and no strategy or
future-return field is available to this experiment.

## Usefulness boundary

MKT-LDR-001 is representation construction only. It cannot establish leader
failure, predict reversal or continuation, authorize a short or breakout veto,
or imply a strategy archetype. A representation failure leaves the broader
leader-failure family open.
