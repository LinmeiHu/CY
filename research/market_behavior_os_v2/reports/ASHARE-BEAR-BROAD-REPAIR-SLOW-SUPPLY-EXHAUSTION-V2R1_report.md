# ASHARE-BEAR-BROAD-REPAIR-SLOW-SUPPLY-EXHAUSTION-V2R1

`LATER_PERIOD_GATE_FAILS`

## Frozen interpretation tested

The complete 1,765-chart review was compressed before replay into: exact causal BEAR, same-day broad cross-sectional repair, exact slow-supply-exhaustion stock signal, next legal open, +10%/H20, and sale at the next legal open after two consecutive closes below the signal-time frozen prior-five-session high.

All market-state values were available by the signal close. No future market return was used. No 2021 signal or 2022-2024 signal/outcome was read.

## Result

The broad-repair condition retained 1,557 candidates on 134 signal dates. Of 1,554 completed trades, mean net return was 3.18%, median 9.60%, win rate 57.08%, and severe-loss incidence 3.80%. The same filtered candidates under the unchanged mother exit had 1,551 completions, 4.94% mean, 9.60% median, 75.50% win rate, and 6.00% severe-loss incidence.

The platform-failure exit affected 652 trades. It shortened mean holding from 13.09 to 8.41 sessions and reduced severe losses by 2.20 percentage points, but reduced mean return by 1.75 percentage points. It is defensive information, not an Alpha improvement in this translation.

The written count and pooled-mean gates both fail. The exact rule is closed without threshold, confirmation, or exit rescue.

## Interpretation

Chart-level platform loss does identify risk, but a binary two-close exit cannot distinguish a normal retest from an ultimately failed repair soon enough. More fundamentally, raw candidate count is concentrated into a small number of market episodes; it does not provide uniformly independent opportunities across years.
