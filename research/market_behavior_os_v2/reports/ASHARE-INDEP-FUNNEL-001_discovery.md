## Interpretation

Seven frozen families were screened on one shared PIT panel. Executable promotion was limited to none. Passing a screen is only a development prioritization decision; failure closes only the exact representation/translation, not the broader mechanism family.

What market behavior are we still not studying? Signal-time intraday supply/demand within these independent families, non-price fundamental revision, order-book pressure, borrow-feasible short legs, and genuinely untouched temporal confirmation.

Has any discovered mechanism implied a genuinely new strategy archetype? No family cleared the fixed standalone promotion boundary in this batch.
