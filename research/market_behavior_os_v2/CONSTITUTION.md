## Scientific invariants

1. Representation stability is not strategy usefulness. In particular,
   MKT-TRND-001 freezes trend direction only as a representation; it establishes
   no trading signal or strategy-habitat predictor.
2. Absolute cross-year-comparable values remain primary. Strictly PIT historical
   context and contemporaneous relative views are additional coordinates, never
   replacements for the absolute state.
3. Unknown required lineage, timestamps, adjustment semantics, or execution
   facts fail closed. A completed bar can affect only a later decision or fill.
4. Failed experiments, exact representations, hypotheses, mechanisms, and broad
   research families are distinct statuses. None is silently promoted to another.
5. One primary frontier and at most one necessary supporting frontier may be
   active. A synthesis decision precedes a major reallocation.
6. Mechanisms precede combinations. At most two distinct mechanisms enter an
   initial combination; incrementality, redundancy, conditional synergy, right-
   tail preservation, and failure behavior must be explicit.
7. CY-011 remains locked and unopened. No current task requires it.

## Durability and stop boundary

Every material scientific experiment updates the state, ledgers, report, and
handoff before a safe commit and push. Ordinary experiment completion is not a
stop. Only the recorded S1-S12 integrity, PIT, locked-data, production, resource,
validation, low-information-value, or human-governance boundaries permit STOP.
