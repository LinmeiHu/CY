## Scientific invariants

1. Representation stability is not strategy usefulness. In particular,
   MKT-TRND-001 freezes trend direction only as a representation; it establishes
   no trading signal or strategy-habitat predictor.
2. Absolute cross-year-comparable values remain primary. Strictly PIT historical
   context and contemporaneous relative views are additional coordinates, never
   replacements for the absolute state.
3. Unknown required lineage, timestamps, adjustment semantics, or execution
   facts fail closed. A completed bar can affect only a later decision or fill.
4. Failed experiments, exact representations, hypotheses, mechanisms, and broad
   research families are distinct statuses. None is silently promoted to another.
5. One primary frontier and at most one necessary supporting frontier may be
   active. A synthesis decision precedes a major reallocation.
6. Mechanisms precede combinations. At most two distinct mechanisms enter an
   initial combination; incrementality, redundancy, conditional synergy, right-
   tail preservation, and failure behavior must be explicit.
7. CY-011 remains locked and unopened. No current task requires it.

## Research levels and operating rule

- `EXPLORE`: governed data, PIT/sample sanity, a clear definition, effect-size
  evidence, and an honest exploratory label. Formal preregistration, LOYO,
  placebo, replication, and byte reruns are optional unless they answer a real
  correctness or decision question.
- `PROMOTE`: freeze the core hypothesis when evidence may change an accepted
  mechanism, Market State Engine, habitat, candidate ranking, failure veto,
  combination architecture, or strategy design. Use only robustness/confound
  tests capable of changing that decision.
- `CONFIRM`: temporal OOS, execution, costs, capacity, orthogonal replication,
  and governed holdout work are reserved for formal habitats, production-
  relevant components, serious strategy candidates, or portfolio routing.

Explore cheaply, promote selectively, and confirm heavily. Exact IDs, dates,
membership, counts, protected hashes, PIT, universe, timestamps, and execution
semantics remain hard gates. Floating aggregates use a frozen strict numeric-
equivalence rule unless bit identity is itself scientific semantics; ULP-scale
arithmetic-order noise cannot stop otherwise valid research.

## Parallel governance

Scientific decisions remain centralized in the Director. Each writing worker
uses an isolated branch/worktree and writes only its worker-packet namespace.
Only the Director changes authoritative state. Resource allocation follows the
measured `RESOURCE_PROFILE.md` and `GLOBAL_RESOURCE_BUDGET.md`; parallelism may
increase throughput but may not multiply hypothesis search.

Separate worktrees are required only for concurrent tracked-file writers.
Read-only analysis and isolated cache/output work may use simpler isolation.
Minute infrastructure stays limited to the reusable security-session ledger and
lossless 241-position primitives until multiple real consumers justify another
durable primitive.

## Durability and stop boundary

An ordinary experiment updates its report or ledger. A material finding updates
STATE and the current frontier. HANDOFF changes only for major synthesis, STOP,
or human handoff. Do not mechanically update every registry or rerun every
validation method. Ordinary experiment completion is not a stop. Only recorded
integrity, PIT, locked-data, production, resource, validation, low-information-
value, or human-governance boundaries permit STOP.
