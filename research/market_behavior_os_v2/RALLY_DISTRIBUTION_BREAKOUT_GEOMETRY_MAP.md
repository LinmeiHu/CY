# Rally-effort distribution versus objective-crossing geometry map

Frozen before MKT-MIN-AD-GEO-001 estimates any cross-family association. The
study asks whether the accepted same-session rally-effort-distribution score is
a manifestation of full-market objective prior-high formation/closing state or
a distinct completed-session coordinate. It does not test future reversal,
economic usefulness, or a strategy.

## Bound representations and clock

MKT-MIN-AD-001 retains `rally_effort_distribution`, available at 15:30 after
the completed 15:00 minute bar. MKT-BREAKOUT-DIFF-001 retains seven objective-
crossing levels available at 15:00: formation participation, formation depth,
closing acceptance, closing rejection depth, formation diffusion, formation
leadership concentration, and stock/industry divergence.

Join exact trade-date, governed-view, and denominator keys. The joint state is
available only at the later 15:30 timestamp. No same-session action, future
value, raw security/minute row, strategy field, post-2023 row, or CY-011 may be
read.

## Coordinate systems

The rally score is intrinsically a causal-percentile composite, so its primary
level coordinate is already causal PIT-normalized. Compare it with each breakout
role in three fixed coordinate systems:

1. causal PIT: rally score versus the breakout role's trailing-756 PIT
   percentile;
2. relative-to-ALL_A: same-date view-minus-ALL_A coordinates;
3. governed-view rank: same-date ranks, pooled across all four views within
   denominator so the estimand preserves cross-view ordering.

For ordinary open-to-close return, construct the same causal-PIT, raw
view-minus-ALL_A, and governed-view-rank alternatives. The accepted VWAP-
defense score supplies its already frozen PIT, relative-to-ALL_A, and rank
coordinates.

## Pairwise and joint geometry

Estimate pairwise Spearman association between rally distribution and every
breakout role separately in fixed blocks A=2018--2020 and B=2021--2023. Causal-
PIT and relative-to-ALL_A estimates are made within each eligible view/
denominator cell; governed-view rank is pooled within denominator. Every cell
requires 150 complete observations.

Then reconstruct the rally score by rank OLS from all seven breakout roles plus
open-to-close return and accepted VWAP defense. All nine controls are mandatory.
Pairwise absolute rho at or above 0.85 or joint adjusted R2 at or above 0.70
median/0.85 maximum means the rally score is not a distinct engine dimension in
that geometry. Both blocks and all coordinates are conjunctive; no favorable
role, block, view, denominator, coordinate, or control deletion can rescue a
failure.

The primary semantic contrast is closing rejection versus closing acceptance,
but those roles receive no selection privilege and all seven are reported. No
interaction product, threshold, state bin, resistance score, or latent composite
is constructed.

## Claim boundary

Passing establishes only contemporaneous nonredundancy among completed-session
representations. Failing establishes only that the rally score is reconstructable
from the fixed geometry. Neither result establishes resistance, participant
distribution, causality, future rejection/reversal, prediction, habitat,
timing, execution, payoff, capacity, or a strategy archetype.
