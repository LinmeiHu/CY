# Circulating-size external market-engine geometry map

Frozen before MKT-STYLE-GEO-001 estimates any cross-family correlation or joint
reconstruction. The study asks whether the six internally stable circulating-
size roles are direct engine coordinates or manifestations of already accepted
market states. It reads no future value or strategy outcome.

Every control is an accepted representation or a direct coordinate surviving a
prior external-engine compression. Failed breadth acceleration, failed liquidity
change, failed volatility term structure, and compressed industry participation/
dispersion are prohibited.

## Coordinates and support

Join exact date/view/denominator keys. Preserve four coordinates for every role
and control: raw absolute, trailing causal 3-year percentile, relative-to-ALL_A,
and governed-view relative rank. Relative-to-ALL_A excludes the deterministic
ALL_A zero group. No missing group, date, or control may be substituted.

All matched 2021--2023 view/denominator/year cells require at least 150 complete,
nondegenerate observations before any correlation or regression. The complete
support audit must finish before estimates.

## Pairwise and joint gates

For each size role and coordinate:

- calculate within-group Spearman against every fixed control;
- require every control's median absolute Spearman across groups below 0.85;
- rank-transform within group and regress the size role jointly on all fixed
  controls plus an intercept;
- require median adjusted R2 across groups below 0.70 and maximum below 0.85.

All four coordinates are conjunctive. A role failing any pairwise or joint gate
is retained as a stable size manifestation but not counted as a direct engine
dimension. No control deletion, coordinate selection, view/year subset, residual
redefinition, or threshold change may rescue it.

establish causality, a small-cap premium, risk appetite, temporal dynamics,
future return, strategy habitat, timing, execution, capacity, or a rule. No
Trend x Breadth x Style interaction is authorized. Future fields, strategy
outcomes, failed roles, post-2023 data, and CY-011 are prohibited.
