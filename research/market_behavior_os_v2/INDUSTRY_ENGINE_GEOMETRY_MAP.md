# Industry/relative-strength external geometry map

Frozen before MKT-INDRS-GEO-001 geometry. This is an outcome-blind compression
study, not a temporal-response, habitat, or strategy experiment.

## Question

Which of the seven MKT-INDRS-001 minimal roles remain distinct after comparison
with economically relevant accepted Market State Engine coordinates?

Internal nonredundancy is not sufficient. Equal-industry participation may be
another central-direction manifestation; industry and within-industry
dispersion may be volatility/co-movement manifestations; winner diffusion and
rank rotation may be broad leadership manifestations; and residual tail/
concentration shapes may reproduce signed risk appetite or market-wide
concentration. These alternatives are tested before any future response.

## Frozen role-specific control sets

| Target role | Accepted controls | Alternative explanation |
|---|---|---|
| equal-industry positive participation | central signed direction; upside extreme participation; downside extreme participation | same-day broad directional breadth |
| industry return dispersion | realized-volatility level; intraday-range level; leave-one-out co-movement | common volatility/correlation state |
| winner-industry diffusion | breadth discovery; broad leadership concentration; leadership/discovery imbalance | already-frozen breadth/leadership geometry |
| industry rank rotation | broad leadership concentration; leave-one-out co-movement; volatility change | ordinary concentration, correlation, or volatility transition |
| stock/industry residual dispersion | leave-one-out co-movement; realized-volatility level; intraday-range level | common volatility/correlation state after industry removal |
| stock/industry residual tail balance | central signed direction; upside extreme participation; downside extreme participation | broad signed risk appetite |
| stock/industry residual concentration | broad leadership concentration; amount concentration; volatility concentration | generic cross-sectional mass concentration |

The sets are fixed by economic role and contain at most three controls. No
stepwise selection, common master control pool, favorable-control deletion, or
failed representation is allowed.

## Coordinate geometry

Every target/control pair is tested separately in:

1. raw absolute values;
2. causal trailing-three-year percentiles after the 504-observation warm-up;
3. same-date non-ALL_A view-minus-ALL_A values;
4. same-date governed-view percentile ranks.

Raw and relative group/year cells use 2019-2023. Causal PIT cells and geometry
use complete 2021-2023 years so the unchanged 150-observation cell gate is not
applied inside warm-up. The full 10,696-row common population remains bound.

Pairwise median absolute Spearman must be strictly below 0.85 for every fixed
control/coordinate view. Fixed-control joint reconstruction uses rank-transformed
OLS with intercept. A target is jointly distinct only when median adjusted R2 is
below 0.70 and maximum adjusted R2 is below 0.85 for every eligible coordinate
family. Joint models are explanatory redundancy diagnostics, not predictions.

## Interpretation

- `DISTINCT_ENGINE_COORDINATE`: all pairwise and joint gates pass.
- `PAIRWISE_REDUNDANT`: at least one fixed accepted control reaches the 0.85
  median edge in an eligible coordinate family.
- `JOINTLY_RECONSTRUCTABLE`: pairwise gates pass but the fixed controls cross a
  joint reconstruction gate.
- `INVALID`: input identity, availability, key-set, coverage, cell, or numeric
  validity fails before classification.

No favorable view, denominator, year, coordinate, or subset can rescue a role.
A role that is externally redundant remains valid evidence about a latent
market mechanism but is removed from the direct engine panel.

## Claim boundary

Passing establishes contemporaneous state distinctness only. It does not
establish future leadership persistence, market return, stock-selection alpha,
industry rotation timing, habitat fitness, causality, or a trading strategy.
Future values, market returns, strategy outcomes, failed industry roles, failed
MA industry fields, and CY-011 are prohibited.
