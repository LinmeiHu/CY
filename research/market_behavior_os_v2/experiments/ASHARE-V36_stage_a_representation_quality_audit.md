There is a material 2019-to-2020 level shift: the candidate-score two-sample KS distance is 0.2742, and the representation-panel distance is 0.1438. The absolute lag contribution is much more stable than the ratio: candidate median incremental R2 is about 0.104 in both years, while restricted and unrestricted fit both fall in 2020. This supports a genuine lag-information component but makes the score's cross-year level nonstationary.

There is no exact endpoint pile: zero rows equal exactly 0 or 1 in either artifact; all 23,354 panel values and all 531 candidate values are unique. The panel range is 0.00027699 to 0.99999997 and the candidate range is 0.12513717 to 0.99999997. The empirical upper 5% of candidates (27 rows; descriptive only, not a new gate) has median unrestricted R2 0.0469 and median incremental R2 0.0468, with minima 0.00743 and 0.00743. Thus the typical candidate is not based on a vanishing unrestricted fit, but the extreme near-one tail is partly generated by an almost-zero restricted R2 and remains vulnerable to ratio/noise amplification.

Across the full representation panel, delay has Spearman correlation -0.600 with unrestricted R2 and +0.827 with incremental R2. Among selected candidates, those correlations are -0.616 and +0.010 respectively: industry-max selection largely equalizes the absolute increment while favoring a smaller contemporaneous fit. That is consistent with the canonical price-delay construction, but it is also why the near-one tail should not be interpreted as a continuously stronger economic signal without later evidence.

All same-date diagnostics cover all 22 dates. All within-industry diagnostics cover all 22 dates; five controls cover all 952 date-industry groups. Trading continuity has only 389 nonconstant supported groups, an important limitation of that particular cross-sectional diagnostic. Annual Amihud is non-null for 22,270/23,354 panel rows and 477/531 candidates, so its audit coverage is high but not complete.

Additional natural-boundary checks (no fitted threshold) show:

- 502/531 candidates have full trading continuity over the latest 60 sessions; 29 do not. Candidate delay/continuity Spearman is -0.099.
- 433/531 candidates have full average weekly trading continuity over the 56-week regression history; 98 do not. Candidate delay/history-continuity Spearman is -0.094.
- In the empirical upper 5% delay tail, 3/27 lack full recent-60 continuity and 6/27 lack full history continuity.
- Candidate median 60-session turnover is 0.02755 versus 0.02484 in the representation panel, and delay/turnover correlations are small. Together with the current-date median-amount gate (zero failures), this rejects a simple low-liquidity explanation.
- Candidate median idiosyncratic volatility is 0.02762 versus 0.02455 in the panel, and the frozen idiosyncratic-volatility correlations are positive. Therefore V36 is not Low-Idio in disguise; however, the moderate positive loading means high-idiosyncratic-noise amplification is not fully excluded.

The representation is therefore recognizably the frozen canonical price-delay ratio, not merely low R2, suspension, low liquidity, low turnover, momentum, size, or low-volatility under the available outcome-blind diagnostics. It is not completely purified from numerical/noise effects: the near-one tail can have weak absolute unrestricted fit, a minority of candidates have incomplete trading continuity, and the score distribution shifts materially in 2020.

## Decision

**Final classification: `QUALIFIED_WITH_LIMITATIONS`.**

**Proceed with the 531-chart blind visual review: YES.** The representation is exact, broad, deterministic, nonduplicative against all six frozen controls, and typically supported by a material incremental R2. The chart review is justified specifically to test whether the selected morphology is coherent price discovery or instead reveals stale/suspension paths, isolated jumps, or visually noisy extremes. It must remain blind and descriptive; Stage A alone establishes representation quality, not return Alpha or compensation.
