## Review contract

- Mother signal: a PIT-observable increase of at least 50% in `circulating_shares`, with same-day corporate actions excluded and action-coordinate lineage unchanged.
- Signal dates: 2018-01-01 through 2020-12-31 only.
- Charts: 126 market sessions before and after the event, with all context capped at 2021-06-30.
- Review order: chronological, not grouped by outcome.
- Coverage: all 119 chronological contact sheets were inspected, covering all 1,068 frozen events. No sheet or event was sampled away.
- Labels visible during review: fixed H120 anatomy labels prepared after the outcome-blind mother freeze. This is chart-guided development, not independent validation.
- Prohibited during review: 2022-2024 signal identity or outcome access, parameter grids, selecting a favorite year, or changing the mother threshold.

## What the complete pass showed

The supply increase is a useful *economic event anchor*, but it has no unconditional long direction. A large increase, a positive event candle, a high-turnover event, a stock near a low, a stock in an uptrend, and a static BULL market state all have abundant winner and loser counterexamples.

The only morphology that recurred across the full sample was conditional and post-event: after the new supply becomes observable, some stocks hold the event area and later reassert demand above it, while others fail to recover the event area or lose it persistently. The 2020 July-December charts are especially important because they reject the tempting shortcut “BULL market plus supply event”: many events lost heavily despite a favorable broad label.

This does not establish that event-level acceptance is profitable. It only earns one frozen, event-specific test in which absorption, demand reassertion, causal market state, and executable failure handling are defined before aggregation.

## Candidate observations that were rejected before rule freeze

1. **Bigger supply expansion is better or worse.** Both directions have large counterexamples; magnitude remains only the mother-event threshold.
2. **A positive event candle proves absorption.** Many positive event candles precede severe losses.
3. **Only buy events near a local low.** Some winners begin near lows, but many such names continue falling and many winners begin in established advances.
4. **Avoid every event after a strong prior advance.** July 2020 made this tempting, but later winners such as 0924, 0935, and 0944 falsify the blanket rule.
5. **A static BULL regime is enough.** July-December 2020 supplies many direct counterexamples.
6. **Turnover expansion alone proves demand.** High and low event turnover both occur among winners and losers.
7. **Use the same generic event-corridor rule as the prior size experiment.** Rejected as a repeat. The retained experiment must be specifically anchored to a causally observed supply expansion and require stock-versus-industry demand plus market state at the later trigger.

## Compressed economic interpretation

The event creates an observable supply test. It becomes a long candidate only if three things happen in causal order:

1. the added supply does not immediately overwhelm the event low;
2. the stock later closes above the event high while outperforming its PIT industry over the same post-event interval;
3. the broad market is already favorable or is causally repairing at that later decision time.

Failure after entry is defined against the original supply-event low, not an optimized trailing technical level. This is a single falsifiable translation, not proof that the visual interpretation is true.
