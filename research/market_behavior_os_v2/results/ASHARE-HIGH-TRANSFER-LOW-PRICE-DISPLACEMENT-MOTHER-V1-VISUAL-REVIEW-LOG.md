# ASHARE-HIGH-TRANSFER-LOW-PRICE-DISPLACEMENT-MOTHER-V1 visual review log

## Why this family is being studied

The repository-wide deduplication review closed the exact annual Amihud,
long-suspension reopening, seller-anchor reclaim, momentum, 52-week-high,
industry-momentum, MAX/MIN, low-risk, simple reversal, gap-repair, support,
price-limit, and adjacent exact families. This experiment asks a different
observable question: what happens after a stock transfers unusually much of
its free float but its completed close barely moves?

This is not called accumulation or absorption. Daily OHLCV cannot identify
buyer or seller intent. The direction remains unknown until the complete chart
corpus has been reviewed.

## Frozen mother event

- Full signal years: 2015 through 2019.
- Exactly 60 strictly prior valid tradable same-lineage sessions.
- Signal turnover is at least 5% of free float and at least 3 times its prior-60
  median.
- Absolute coordinate close-to-close return is no more than 1%.
- No return, trend, market-state, industry, or intraday-range direction filter.
- After an admitted event, the next 20 market-session indices for that symbol
  are suppressed causally.
- Decision time is the completed signal-session close; same-bar entry is
  forbidden.
- No 2021 or later signal, chart, or outcome has been read.

## Full-corpus compression checkpoint

All 680 chronological sheets and all 6,115 frozen events have been reviewed
exactly once. The stable visual result is not a new candlestick shape. It is a
single causal lifecycle:

1. The high-transfer/low-displacement mother event is only an observation seed.
2. The seed must expire quickly unless price repeatedly accepts above its
   pre-known event high.
3. The common market state must be observable at confirmation time and may
   change admission probability, but it cannot replace stock-level acceptance.
4. Loss of the accepted event-high area after entry is the natural structural
   invalidation.
5. The trade needs a finite life because later bull-market moves cannot be
   credited to a stale mother event.

freezing it, the renderer's BULL/TRANSITION/BEAR source and the repository's V26
market-state precedent must be inspected to ensure that the final state rule is

## Outcome-group contradiction check

After the full chronological review, five evenly spaced `PROFIT_GE_4PCT` sheets
and five evenly spaced `SEVERE_LOSS` sheets were inspected. This step challenged
the proposed distinctions; it did not generate additional thresholds.

- The profit group contains many stale endpoint winners. They remain below the
  event structure for weeks or months and rise only in a later common-market
  advance. A short confirmation expiry will intentionally reject these labels.
- The severe-loss group contains many one-session crosses or event-day spikes
  above the event high followed by an immediate failure. Repeated completed
  closes are necessary; a single breakout is contradicted by the loss group.
- Some severe H120 losers genuinely accept and advance first, then lose the
  event-high area. This contradicts confirmation-only buy-and-hold and preserves
  the need for a structural exit.
- Both outcome groups contain BULL, BEAR, and TRANSITION labels. A state label
  alone is not a stock-selection rule. It can only govern whether a confirmed
  stock has enough common opportunity to be admitted.
- The contradiction check did not reveal a reliable event-candle, turnover,
  range, prior-return, or close-location discriminator. Those proposed extra
  indicators remain rejected.

The V26 precedent was also inspected. Its useful hierarchy is a pre-existing
objective structure followed by a separate causal trigger; its numeric corridor
and gap-width rules are specific to that strategy and are not reusable here.
The existing market-state asset is causal at the completed close: BULL requires
both 20- and 60-session cross-sectional median returns above zero and both
positive-return shares above 50%; BEAR requires all four measures on the other
side; mixed cases are TRANSITION. No future interval or future extremum enters
that state.
