# Market Behavior Research OS V2 contract

Effective 2026-08-30. This contract supersedes the assumption that CHINEXT V1
is the research universe. It does not revise or erase evidence produced under
the seed-strategy programs.

## Research object and tracks

The primary research object is recurring market behavior. Work is divided into
three independently visible tracks:

1. `MARKET`: strategy-agnostic state, process, and transition research;
2. `ARCHAEOLOGY`: existing strategies as seeds, benchmarks, and case studies;
3. `INVENTION`: minimum executable architectures derived from supported market
   mechanisms.

No track may silently substitute for another. In particular, an association
with CHINEXT V1 outcomes does not define a market state, and a plausible market
process does not by itself authorize a strategy prototype.

## Scientific progression

The progression is:

`market representation -> recurring process -> mechanism -> independent
replication -> executable archetype -> temporal validation -> habitat mapping ->
production eligibility`.

Most representations and proposed strategies are expected to fail. A new
archetype requires a coherent mechanism; a prototype requires preliminary
evidence; a candidate requires robustness and temporal evidence; production
eligibility requires the repository's stronger execution, tail, capacity,
governance, and untouched-validation gates.

## State coordinates

Every important state family distinguishes:

- absolute economic state;
- causal PIT historical normalization;
- contemporaneous relative state where an authorized comparison set exists.

Relative rank never replaces absolute state. Expanding or rolling historical
normalization uses only completed observations available at `decision_at`.
Unknown lineage or insufficient causal history is missing and fails closed.

## PIT, execution, and data governance

- Preserve `available_at`, `snapshot_id`, exact asset identity, and hard-valid
  semantics.
- A completed bar can first affect a later executable timestamp; no signal can
  fill inside its formation bar.
- Suspensions, limits, auctions, lunch breaks, corporate actions, and abnormal
  or missing bars retain their registered semantics.
- No unregistered input or silent fallback is permitted.
- Post-entry observations are attribution labels at their declared timestamps,
  never entry predictors.
- Market-only construction experiments may not read strategy returns, trades,
  MFE, MAE, exits, or outcome classes.

## Holdouts and consumed evidence

All 2018-2025 CHINEXT outcomes already inspected by earlier programs are
exploratory PIT-B evidence, not untouched confirmation. `CY-011` remains unopened
and unavailable to this program. A market-only terminal preview of five 2026
`399102` rows occurred during the scope migration; it contained no CY-011 data,
security data, trades, labels, or strategy outcomes. Those rows are quarantined
from research, and the active construction cutoff is 2023-12-31.

## Ledgers and status vocabulary

Market hypotheses and strategy families have separate frontiers. Every
outcome-bearing experiment and every tested combination receives a frozen spec
before association. Strategy-library statuses are:

`PROPOSED`, `EXPLORATORY`, `SUPPORTED_EXPLORATORY`, `VALIDATING`, `REJECTED`,
`CANDIDATE`, and `PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE`.

## Stop boundary

Stop on unknown required lineage, required locked-data access, indispensable
unavailable inputs, unsafe resource use, destructive Git requirements, or a
canonical/production change without authorization. A negative result is not a
stop condition.
