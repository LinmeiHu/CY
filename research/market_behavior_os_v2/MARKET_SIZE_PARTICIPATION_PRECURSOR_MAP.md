# Circulating-size participation precursor map

Frozen before MKT-STYLE-PART-DYN-001 shifts any future state. This is the one
bounded cross-mechanism edge allowed after the exact leadership-transition
self-process failed. It asks whether current small-versus-large positive
participation breadth precedes the future accepted leadership transition after
fixed current-state controls.

## Primary edge

The predictor is the accepted small30-minus-large30 positive-return fraction at
t. The response is the accepted five-session leadership-transition coordinate
at t+5:

`T5(t+5) = L20(t+5) - L20(t)`.

The predictor's same-session t return does not enter either daily size-return
component of the response. Passing positive association would mean small-minus-
large participation breadth precedes strengthening small-size leadership;
passing negative association would mean it precedes weakening. The sign is
learned once from reused block A and must reproduce everywhere else.

The experiment studies a future market size state, not future market payoff or
a strategy return.

## Fixed predictor neighbors

Use small20-minus-large20 and small40-minus-large40 positive participation as
raw definition challenges. Both predict the same raw t+5 accepted transition.
They must support the primary sign in both blocks and cannot rescue a failed
small30-minus-large30 primary. No other size role or edge enters.

## Fixed current controls

Partial-rank estimation uses exactly:

- current accepted five-session leadership transition;
- current accepted size-curve divergence;
- current accepted broad central direction from signed limit utilization.

These separate the precursor from current transition state, cross-size return
amplitude, and generic signed market direction. No control may be deleted.

## Coordinates, blocks, and phase

The primary predictor, response, and controls preserve raw, causal three-year
PIT percentile, relative-to-ALL_A, and governed-view rank coordinates. Relative
rank uses matched same-date four-view values with date fixed effects. The 20/40
predictor neighbors are raw-only because their own normalized/relative
coordinates were not frozen.

Both blocks are already-consumed exploratory time:

- reused block A: 2021-01-01 through 2021-12-31;
- reused block B: 2022-01-01 through 2023-12-31.

Predictor and response dates must both lie in the block. A phase-zero sample
takes every fifth valid predictor within each original group/block so future
five-session response intervals do not overlap across observations.

## Frozen gates

Support requires at least 150 complete observations per ordinary group/block,
600 pooled four-view observations per relative-rank denominator/block, and 40
per phase-zero group/block.

The primary requires raw median absolute partial Spearman at least 0.10 in both
blocks, the learned sign in six of eight groups, and block-B magnitude at least
half block A. Phase-zero requires 0.08 and six-of-eight sign support. PIT
requires 0.08 and six-of-eight support. Relative-to-ALL_A requires 0.05 and five
of six; corrected relative rank requires 0.05 and both denominators. Each raw
20/40 predictor neighbor requires 0.06 and six-of-eight learned-sign support in
both blocks.

All gates are conjunctive. No sign, tail definition, horizon, block,
coordinate, phase, view, denominator, control deletion, additional edge,
failed role, or favorable subset can rescue failure.

## Claim boundary

Passing establishes only an outcome-blind size-participation precursor to a
future accepted size state. It does not establish a small-cap premium, future
market payoff, stock selection, risk appetite, habitat fitness, timing,
execution, capacity, causality, or a strategy rule. Post-2023 data, strategy
outcomes, and CY-011 are prohibited.
