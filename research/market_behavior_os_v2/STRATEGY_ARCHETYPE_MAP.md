# Strategy archetype map

This is a search map, not an implementation backlog. An archetype enters the
strategy library only after a coherent mechanism and enough preliminary evidence
exist to justify a family record.

| Archetype | Candidate recurring process | Distinctive trigger/exit implication | Current basis | Map status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakout | supply clears and price is accepted beyond objective resistance | acceptance trigger; rejection/failure exit | CHINEXT seed; formation evidence mixed | REPRESENTED_BY_SEED |
| Trend continuation | established quality trend survives consolidation | reacceleration trigger; deterioration exit | market mechanism unbuilt | SEARCH_SPACE |
| Trend acceleration | trend strength/participation transition upward | transition trigger; deceleration exit | untested | SEARCH_SPACE |
| Pullback continuation | controlled retracement exhausts supply within persistent trend | recovery/reacceleration trigger; support failure exit | coherent hypothesis only | SEARCH_SPACE |
| Relative-strength leadership | persistent relative demand diffuses or reconcentrates in leaders | renewed leadership trigger; relative failure exit | seven industry/stock-relative state coordinates freeze; external geometry, temporal meaning, trigger, and outcome evidence absent | SEARCH_SPACE |
| Cross-sectional dispersion / relative value | high cross-industry disagreement precedes a wider next 1/3/5-session security-return distribution without directional mean shift | fixed PIT continuation or reversal ranking; convergence/risk-budget exit remains unknown | post-discovery EXPLORE is positive across cells, years, neighbors, and controls; two exact rank-build implementations failed different resource ceilings and produced no ranking/PnL/cost/capacity evidence | EXPLORE_CANDIDATE_RESOURCE_PARKED |
| Mean reversion | displacement exceeds persistent information and normalizes | reversal confirmation; normalization exit | untested | SEARCH_SPACE |

No archetype is currently authorized for implementation. Dispersion/relative
value remains the sole genuinely new research archetype; both newly executable
admission-veto translations failed their fixed promotion rules.
