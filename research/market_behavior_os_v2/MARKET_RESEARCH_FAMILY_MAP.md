# Strategy-agnostic market research family map

Statuses describe breadth of market-process evidence, not usefulness to one
strategy.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Volatility | level, term structure, contraction, expansion, downside asymmetry | Basic index/stock levels exist; conditional process weakly studied | FAMILY_UNDEREXPLORED | State transitions conditional on trend/breadth |

they do not close the encompassing market family.
